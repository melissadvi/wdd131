WDD131 Dynamic Web Fundamentals
Project Week 04: Website Subject
Author: Melissa Adão Vieira

01. Subject: An informational website about Spider-Man's movies, comparing the 
different versions of the character across film universes.

02. Content: A home page introducing the character, a movies page with an 
interactive table that can be filtered by universe or actor and sorted by release 
year, a villains page and a contact page with a form for fan polls. Users will be 
able to mark movies as watched, saved in their browser. A references page will credit
all sources.

03. Reason: Spider-Man is my favorite superhero, and many people are 
be confuse by different movie versions. I want to build a clear, organized guide that helps
them explore each version.
